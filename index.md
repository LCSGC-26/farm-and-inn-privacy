# Privacy Policy for Farm & Inn

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

This policy covers the Farm & Inn app for Android, made by Luminist Coast
Studio. It explains what the game does with your information.

## What the game stores

Farm & Inn saves your game (your farm, your inn, your bag, your coins, your
level and your settings) in the app's own storage **on your device**.

That save never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not backed up to
us or synced between devices, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What we collect

**We collect nothing ourselves.** There is no account, no sign-in, no
analytics and no crash reporting of our own. The game does not send us any
information about you or how you play. Apart from loading ads, it works with
no network connection.

## Advertising

Farm & Inn shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**:

- a full-screen ad, at most once per in-game day, when you continue from the
  end-of-day summary
- optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for a bonus (double
  tips, or a free seed bundle)

To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process data from your
device, including your device's advertising ID, your approximate location
(worked out from your IP address), information about your device and the
app, how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google does this
processing, not us; we never receive or store that data. It is sent over an
encrypted connection. Google explains its practices here:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites (how Google uses
  information from apps that use its services)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
the game shows you Google's consent form before requesting any ad and follows
your choice. You can change your choice at any time from **Menu > Settings >
Privacy choices**. If you decline, the game works exactly the same; you see
non-personalised ads instead.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's
settings, under **Settings > Google > Ads** (or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**
on some phones).

## Children

Farm & Inn is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at
children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to show,
correct or delete. To remove everything the game has stored, start a new game
from the title screen, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or
uninstall the app. To ask Google to delete data linked to your advertising
ID, reset or delete that ID as described above.
